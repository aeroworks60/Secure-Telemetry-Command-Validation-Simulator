04 / FRESHNESS & REPLAY

Time and order

Timestamp window

Maximum age: 120 seconds. Future timestamps more than 30 seconds ahead are rejected.

Sequence progression

For each satellite ID, a successfully accepted sequence must be greater than the last accepted one.

Duplicate identifiers

Message IDs seen earlier in the running process are rejected if reused, including IDs from previously rejected messages.

!
05 / SCOPE BOUNDARY

What this does not prove

  • A valid timestamp does not authenticate a sender.
  • A duplicate-ID check is not complete replay protection by itself.
  • Role checks are demo policy, not real identity management.
  • HMAC proves a message was not changed since signing by a holder of the shared key. It does not encrypt the message, hide its contents, or make this prototype production-secure.
  • The demo signing key is a development-only value unless SENTINEL_HMAC_KEY is set on the server.
  • This app does not communicate with a satellite.
